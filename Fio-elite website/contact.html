Before you reach out

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

A few things clients commonly want to know before starting a project with us.

What information should I include in my enquiry? +
Include the type of service you need, approximate quantity, dimensions, preferred materials, artwork availability and deadline whenever possible.
Can you handle both design and production? +
Yes. Fio Elite can support projects across creative visual design, printing, corporate branding, large-format production and exhibition or event branding.
Do you work with businesses across the UAE? +
Yes. We are based in Dubai and can support advertising, printing, branding and production requirements across the UAE.
Can I request a quotation before starting? +
Yes. Send us your project requirements through the enquiry form or by email and we can review the scope and prepare the appropriate quotation.
Can you handle urgent projects? +
Urgent requirements can be discussed with our team. Availability depends on the project scope, production requirements and deadline.
Where are you located? +
Fio Elite Advertising L.L.C. is located at Plaza Building, Shop No. 7, 44A Street, Hor Al Anz, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.
